      What do the letters mean?

      R U F L D B turn the Right, Up (top), Front, Left, Down (bottom) or Back face clockwise, as if you were looking straight at that face.

      R' (with a dash) turns it anticlockwise. R2 turns it twice.

      Lowercase r f turn two layers at once. M is the middle slice, turned like L.

      x y z rotate the whole cube: y like turning the top, x like R, z like F.
