Record a test session

This records video of you turning the cube, plus exactly which move you made and when. It's the answer key we use to train and score the live tracker.

  1. Solve your cube and hold it with white on top, green facing the camera.
  2. Press Start. Hold it still for the 3-second countdown.
  3. Do the move shown, then tap Space. Repeat.
  4. Messed up a move? Press Esc. Everything before it is still useful.

Tip: try a few sessions in different light (day, night, lamp). Variety makes the tracker robust.
